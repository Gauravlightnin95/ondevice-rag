## Q7
- **type:** 6
- **question:** Summarise the email security guidance given to users regarding suspicious mail and attachments.
- **answer:** Users should not open any mail or attachment from an unknown or suspicious source. Even where the source is known, if an attachment is of suspicious nature or looks dubious, the user should confirm its authenticity with the sender before opening it, since such messages may contain viruses capable of damaging valuable information on the computer. When sending large attachments users should check that the recipient can receive them. Spam mail received in the inbox should be forwarded to the IT support address, as should any mail wrongly stamped as spam. All mails detected as spam go into the SPAM_MAIL folder of the user's account, and users are asked to check these folders periodically for important messages.
- **passage:** User should not open any mail or attachment that is from unknown and suspicious source. Even if it is from known source, and if it contains any attachment that is of suspicious nature or looks dubious, user should get confirmation from the sender about its authenticity before opening it. This is very much essential from the point of security of the user's computer; as such messages may contain viruses that have potential to damage the valuable information on your computer. While sending large attachments to others, user should make sure that the recipient has email facility that allows him to receive such large attachments. Any Spam mail received by the user into INBOX should be forwarded to itsupport@galgotiasuniversity.edu.in. Any mail wrongly stamped as SPAM mail should be forwarded to itsupport@galgotiasuniversity.edu.in. All the mails detected as spam mails go into SPAM_MAIL folder of the respective users' mail accounts. Users are requested to open these folders periodically.
