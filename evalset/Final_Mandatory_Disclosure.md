## Q11
- **type:** 6
- **question:** Summarise the online grievance redressal mechanism, from registration of a complaint through to the final avenue of appeal.
- **answer:** A person registers the grievance through the grievance redressal portal link on the website. It goes immediately to the concerned HOD, who must acknowledge receipt via the portal. The HOD then provides a solution, invalidates the matter, or comments that it falls outside their scope and forwards it to the grievance cell by mail, submitting a decision report within 5 days of receipt; final approval by the GRC is within 7 days. If the complainant is unsatisfied with the HOD's decision, they may appeal to the Central Level Grievance Committee within four days of receiving the reply. Where a complaint is forwarded to the central grievance redressal cell, the member secretary places the matter before the committee, which either endorses the HOD's decision or passes an appropriate solution. The GRC sends its report with recommendations to the university within 15 days of receipt of the complaint, with a copy to the aggrieved party. If still unsatisfied, the complainant may appeal to the Ombudsperson appointed by the affiliating university, AKTU.
- **passage:** Grievance Redressal mechanism for Faculty, staff and Students. Online Grievance Redressal Mechanism 1. Any Person raising a request should register the grievance through the grievance redressal portal link available on the website. 2. The grievance will immediately go to the concerned HOD who needs to acknowledge the receipt of the grievance Via Portal. 3. HOD needs to provide (1) Necessary solution, (2) Invalid the matter (3) comment if the same is not under the scope and forward to grievance cell through mail. The decision report should be submitted by HoD to the grievance redressal cell is within 5 days from receipt of the complaint. The time to final approval by GRC is within 07 days from receipt of the complaint. 4. If the student/faculty/staff is not satisfied with the decision of the concerned grievance HoD, he/she can submit an appeal to the Central Level Grievance Committee within four days from the date of the receipt of the reply from the concerned grievance HoD. 5. If the Complaint is forwarded to the central grievance redressal cell, then the member secretary of the grievance committee will place the matter before the grievance committee members which shall either endorse the decision of the concerned grievance HoD or shall pass appropriate solution in the best possible manner. 6. The GRC shall send its report with recommendations, if any, to the university and a copy thereof to the aggrieved faculty/ staff/ student, within 15 days from the date of receipt of the complaint. 7. In case faculty/staff/student is not satisfied with the decision of the Student Grievance Redressal Committee(SGRC), they may appeal, to the Ombudsperson appointed by the affiliating university (AKTU) for redressal of their grievance.
- **page:** 4
