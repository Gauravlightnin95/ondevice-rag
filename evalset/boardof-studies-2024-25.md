## Q1
- **type:** 3
- **question:** What is the prescribed composition of a Board of Studies, and how many members does it have in total including the chairperson?
- **answer:** Nine members: the Dean of the Faculty as Chairperson, four internal subject experts, two external subject experts, and two experts from corporate, industry or R&D organizations.
- **passage:** The Board shall consist of the following members, i. Dean of the Faculty Chairperson ii. Four Internal Subject Experts (Experts in the ranks of Professor / Asso. Professor / Asst. Prof. from Amity University Chhattisgarh) Members iii. Two External Subject Experts (Experts in the ranks of Professor of other University) Members iv. Two Experts from Corporate / Industry / R&D Organizations in the concerned subject (The members shall be of Senior Management Level) Members

## Q3
- **type:** 2
- **question:** Who proposes the composition of a Board of Studies, through whom, and whose approval is required?
- **answer:** The composition is proposed by the concerned Head, through the Dean of the Faculty, for approval of the Vice-Chancellor.
- **passage:** For each Subject/Discipline/Programme of the University, a Board of Studies shall be constituted which shall be chaired by its Director / Head. The composition of the Board shall be proposed by the concerned Head through the Dean of the Faculty for approval of the Vice-Chancellor.

## Q4
- **type:** 1
- **question:** Under which legislation and statute is the Board of Studies constituted?
- **answer:** In pursuance of Section 25 of the Chhattisgarh Private Universities (Establishment & Operation) (Amendment) Act No. 13 of 2005, as amended by Chhattisgarh Act No. 2014, and empowered by Statute No. 11, Clause (1) of the Amity University, Chhattisgarh.
- **passage:** In Pursuance to the Section 25 of the Chhattisgarh Private Universities (Establishment & Operation) (Amendment) Act No. 13 of 2005, (Amendment of Chhattisgarh Act No. 2014) and being empowered by Statute No. 11, Clause (1) of the Amity University, Chhattisgarh, the Board of Studies is constituted by each School of Studies of the University.
