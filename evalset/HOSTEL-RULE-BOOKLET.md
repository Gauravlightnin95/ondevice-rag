## Q18
- **type:** 6
- **question:** Summarise how the mess is run, who is responsible for food quality, and what happens during holidays.
- **answer:** The mess facility is provided by a Mess Contractor with the consent of a Mess Committee made up of hosteller students. The Mess Committee is responsible for the quality and hygienic conditions of the food served. The college is only responsible for making payment to the Mess Contractor on behalf of hosteller students. Breakfast, lunch, evening tea and snacks, and dinner are provided at stipulated times. The mess is closed during Holi and Diwali holidays and summer and winter vacations, and no refunds are permissible. No mess offs are allowed for any reason. Complaints are to be reported to the Warden or Chief Warden, and students must not argue with mess staff or the contractor.
- **passage:** Mess facility to the students is provided by the Mess Contractor with the consent of Mess Committee comprising of Hosteller Students.
