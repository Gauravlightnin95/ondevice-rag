- **question:** What must a student do after receiving an F grade in a core course, and how does this differ for an elective course?

## Q23
- **type:** 6
- **question:** Summarise the rules governing the Summer Term, including who may register, what limits apply, and what a student gives up by registering.
- **answer:** The Summer Term runs at double the pace of a normal semester, with one credit requiring two hours per week so total contact hours match a normal semester. Only students who have been detained in a course may register, and only for theory category courses, not audit or practical courses. A maximum of four courses is allowed, and students must submit a written willingness in the prescribed format. Attendance of 90% is required, and falling below it bars the student from that course's summer term examination. Registering requires signing an undertaking cancelling all previous CAT/IA internal marks for that subject, with no restoration possible. Once registered, a student cannot withdraw. The summer term is not applicable to courses not registered in previous terms, cannot be demanded as a matter of right, requires a minimum batch size, and does not set a precedent.
- **passage:** Summer Term will be conducted at an accelerated pace and will be at the double the rate of a normal semester e.g. one credit of course shall require two hours/week so that the total contact hours are maintained same as in normal semester. During summer term, students must attend all classes. If student's attendance is less than 90% in any course/tutorial then he/she will not be permitted to take the summer term examination of that course. Summer term, is only for theory (L) category courses and not for audit courses and practical category courses. Only those students who have been detained in any course can register. Students shall be allowed to register for maximum four courses. Those students who are willing to join the summer term will have to submit the "Willingness to join" in the prescribed format. The students who register for the Summer Term will have to sign an undertaking to revoke (cancel) the CAT/ IA marks secured by them in their regular/earlier attempt in the same subject/s. Once registered, students will not be allowed to withdraw from a summer term. The summer term is not applicable to those courses which are not registered in previous terms. Summer term is a special semester and students cannot demand it as a matter of right. Minimum batch size to be required and offering this semester cannot be treated as precedence.
