- **answer:** The tie is first broken by considering the CGPA of the students. If there is a tie in CGPA, it is broken by the candidate's SGPA of the previous two semesters. If the tie still exists, the SGPA of the previous four semesters is considered, and so on.

In case of a tie in CGPA, it shall be broken by considering the candidate's performance on the basis of SGPA of previous two semesters. If, however, the tie still exists, then considering the SGPA of previous four semesters and soon.

## Q22
- **type:** 6
- **question:** Summarise the attendance requirements and the process a student must follow to have an absence authorised.
- **answer:** Students are expected to maintain full attendance, but 75% is the minimum permitted without producing proof of absence. For attendance falling between 75% and 60%, the student must inform the School in advance and seek permission, with the Dean forwarding the case through the Program Chair to the Dean Academics for a final decision. In medical cases the student or parent must inform the Dean through the Program Chair with a doctor's proof and obtain consent within a week of the absence; leave without the Dean's consent is unauthorized. Students representing the University at approved events receive On Duty credit if they apply in writing in advance and obtain the Dean's sanction. Students below 75% are notified by the school and may be debarred from the End Term Examination, though the Dean may seek relaxation in cases of family calamity, prolonged hospitalization, serious accident or other serious unavoidable circumstances, and the Vice Chancellor may grant a 15% relaxation.
- **passage:** A student is expected to maintain full attendance in all courses. Considering the fact that a student may be absent due to ill-health or some family emergency, he/she is permitted to maintain minimum attendance of 75% without producing any proof for the absence. For leave of absence between 75% and 60% of attendance, the student must inform the School in advance and seek permission for absence. The Dean on the recommendation of the Program Chair, will forward such cases to the Dean Academics office for a final decision. In case of medical exigencies, the student/ parent should inform the Dean of school through program Chair with proof from the doctor and obtain the consent within a week, starting from the day of absence. Any leave (of absence) without the consent of the Dean will be treated as unauthorized absence. Students taking leave with the consent of Dean up to 25% (i.e. maintaining a minimum of 75% attendance in each course) will be permitted to write the ETE. Only those students who are nominated/ sponsored by the University to represent in various forums like seminars/ conferences/ workshops/ competitions or taking part in co-curricular/ extra-curricular/placement related activities will be given 'On Duty (OD)' credit provided the student applies in writing for such a leave in advance and obtains sanction from the concerned Dean through respective faculty coordinators. Students who do not meet the requirement of 75% attendance will be notified by the school. In case of attendance falling marginally below 75% due to reasons listed below, the Dean may bring such cases, along with valid and adequate evidence, to the notice of the Dean Academics for relaxation from the minimum 75% condition stated above. (i) Calamity in the immediate family. (ii) Hospitalization due to prolonged in-patient treatment. (iii) Serious accident. (iv) Other serious unavoidable circumstance(s). No minimum attendance percentage criterion will be followed for MTE but for ETE requirement shall be 75% in general. However, the VC shall have a right to give a relaxation of 15% in attendance to eligible students for the students recommended by school deans.
